About

I am a Doctoral Researcher at the University of Mons (UMONS) , supervised by Prof. Nicolas Gillis . My current research sits at the intersection of Matrix Theory and Optimization, specifically developing matrix factorization and low-rank approximation methods for Trustworthy Machine Learning. I aim to leverage these mathematical tools to enhance robustness, interpretability, and fairness in high-dimensional data and network analysis. · · Previously, I was a Graduate Research Assistant at the University of Kurdistan , where I led the Algebraic Machine Learning (AML) team and focused on representation learning.

Research interests

  • Machine Learning: representation learning, deep learning, and unsupervised learning
  • Trustworthy ML: robustness, generalization, interpretability, and fairness
  • Applications: healthcare, recommender systems, and remote sensing
  • Applied Mathematics: linear algebra, optimization, and low-rank approximation
